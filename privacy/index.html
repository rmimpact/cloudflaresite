Website Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you browse this website, contact me or submit a support request. The website does not use advertising or analytics trackers and does not maintain its own database of visitor or support information.

Scope of this policy

This Website Privacy Policy applies to the pages, project pages and support features available on remymoscovitz.com. The website is a personal portfolio operated by Remy Moscovitz.

Some independently developed apps have their own privacy policies because they use device features or information that the website does not. Those app-specific policies appear below and take priority for the relevant app.

Information the website handles

You can browse the website without creating an account or giving Remy your name, email address, phone number, physical address or payment details.

Information is handled only in the following limited situations:

  • information you choose to enter in the support form or send by email;
  • a language preference stored locally in your browser; and
  • standard technical request information processed by hosting, security and content-delivery services.

The website does not use a Remy-operated customer database, advertising platform or analytics service.

Support and contact enquiries

The support form asks for your first and last name, email address, the relevant application, the type of request and your message. This information is used only to deliver, review and respond to your request, investigate a reported issue and maintain necessary correspondence.

Support submissions are not stored in a database on remymoscovitz.com. When you submit the form, the information is sent from your browser to FormSubmit, which forwards it to Remy’s email account. FormSubmit states that it may retain form submissions for 30 days. The resulting email may remain in Remy’s mailbox for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, keep a record of the conversation or meet legal and security requirements.

FormSubmit and the email provider process the information as independent service providers under their own terms and privacy practices. You can review FormSubmit’s privacy terms ↗. If you prefer not to use the form, you can email Remy directly. Please do not send passwords, payment information, private keys, government identifiers, health information or other highly sensitive material.

Technical information

Like most websites, the services that host, secure and deliver this site may automatically process limited technical information when your browser requests a page or file. This can include:

  • your IP address and approximate network location;
  • the requested page or file, date and time of the request;
  • browser, operating-system and device information;
  • referring-page information; and
  • security, error and diagnostic events.

This processing is used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, troubleshoot faults and protect the website. Remy does not use this information to build advertising profiles or track visitors across unrelated websites.

Cookies and local preferences

Remy does not place advertising or analytics cookies on this website. The English and French versions may read your browser’s language and store your chosen language in your browser’s local storage under rm-site-language. This preference remains on your device so the site can show the language you selected.

You can remove the preference at any time by clearing this site’s browser storage. The language links continue to work if local storage is blocked or unavailable.

Third-party websites, downloads and previews

The website links to services and content operated by others, including GitHub, app stores, project websites and software-download hosts. A project page may also load an embedded preview from another website. When you open a third-party link, start a download or load an embedded preview, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the referring page, and may apply its own privacy policy.

Third-party websites and services are not controlled by Remy, and this policy does not describe their independent data practices. Review their privacy information before providing personal information or downloading software.

How information is used and shared

Information handled through the website is used only to:

  • provide pages, files and requested website features;
  • deliver and respond to support or privacy enquiries;
  • investigate faults, abuse and security issues; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Remy does not sell or rent personal information and does not share it for behavioural advertising. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host the site, deliver support messages, provide email or protect the service. It may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the website or another person’s rights and safety.

Some service providers may process information in Australia or other countries where they operate. Their privacy protections and legal requirements may differ from those in your location.

Retention and deletion

The website itself does not keep visitor profiles or a database of support submissions. FormSubmit states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. Support emails are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the enquiry, preserve relevant correspondence and meet legal or security needs, after which they may be deleted.

Technical logs are retained by the relevant hosting and security providers according to their operational and legal requirements. The local language preference remains in your browser until you clear it.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the website and limit the information it handles. However, no website, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit confidential or highly sensitive information through the support form or by ordinary email.

Your choices and privacy requests

You do not need to submit personal information to browse the website. You can contact Remy by email instead of using the support form, clear the saved language preference through your browser and choose whether to open third-party links or previews.

You may ask what personal information Remy holds about you through a support conversation, request a correction or request deletion where applicable. To help locate the relevant correspondence, contact Remy from the same email address used for the original request. Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, support process or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when an updated policy is published. Material new practices will be described before or when they begin to apply.
